s21-lsa-kingdom.png

Title: The LSA kingdom — every LSA type as a character, every flooding scope as a wall it can or cannot cross.

Flat minimalistic but highly detailed cartoon illustration, modern vector-art aesthetic, warm palette: cream background, coral accents, muted gold highlights, sage greens, soft brown outlines, clean bold line work, no gradients, no 3D. Wide landscape composition, slightly elevated three-quarter view of a medieval kingdom drawn as a network diagram. CENTER: a walled capital city labeled "AREA 0 — BACKBONE", its wall gates manned by herald characters in amber sashes labeled "ABR". Around it, three walled villages labeled "AREA 1", "AREA 2", and "AREA 51 — NSSA" (the NSSA village wall has a small mail-slot instead of an open gate), each connected to the capital by a single road — NO roads between villages (one faint, crossed-out dirt path between two villages stamped "FORBIDDEN — backbone rule"). INSIDE each village: (Type 1) every villager holds up a hand-drawn map of their own house and yard, labeled "Type 1 — my own links"; (Type 2) in the village square, ONE town crier wearing a green "DR" medal stands on a podium describing the square itself with a banner "Type 2 — the segment speaks through its DR", while a second character with a "BDR" badge sits silently on a bench holding a rolled-up copy, clearly NOT speaking. AT THE GATES: the amber ABR heralds hold up short scrolls reading "Type 3 — prefix X, through me, cost Y" — pointedly summaries, not maps; a small sign at each gate reads "maps never leave the village". FAR EDGE: outside the kingdom entirely, a foreign merchant caravan labeled "ISP / BGP / static" hands goods to a coral-cloaked smuggler character at the kingdom's edge labeled "ASBR", who launches paper airplanes labeled "Type 5 — external news" that fly OVER all the walls into every standard village (dotted flight paths crossing walls unchanged) — but bounce off the NSSA village and a small "STUB" hamlet, each protected by an umbrella labeled "no Type 5s — default route only". NEAR THE NSSA village: inside its wall, a tiny local smuggler writes on a distinctive coral envelope labeled "Type 7", passes it through the mail-slot to the amber ABR herald, who re-stamps it at a desk labeled "translated: 7 → 5" and launches it onward as a paper airplane. FINALLY: one herald at the capital holds a signpost pointing toward the far-edge smuggler, labeled "Type 4 — directions to the ASBR (written by the ABR!)". Generous spacing, every labeled object reinforcing exactly one LSA concept, educational yet playful.
