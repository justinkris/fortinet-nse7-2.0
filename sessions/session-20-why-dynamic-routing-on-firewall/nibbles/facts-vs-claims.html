The one table that reconstructs the whole session — and the AD ladder that decides ties.

THE COMPARISON

OSPF and BGP Are Different Kinds of Information

OSPF — the IGPBGP — the EGP
What travelsFacts — "this link died" (LSA)Claims — "I can reach X" (revocable)
Who computesEveryone, independently, over an identical database (Dijkstra)Nobody maps anything — claims are believed while the session lives
Runs whereInside one administrationBetween administrations
TransportDirectly on IPTCP port 179
LivenessHello 10s · Dead 40sKeepalive 60s · Hold 180s
On failureFlood the fact → everyone recalculatesExplicit withdrawal · hold expiry flushes the whole peer
The tradeStability sacrificed for speed — small, trusted blast radiusSpeed sacrificed for stability — a flap re-sends an entire table

WRONG KNOB

TTL Is the Fuse, Not the Fix

A distance-vector loop bounces the packet until TTL hits 0 (drop + ICMP Time Exceeded). Nothing is repaired — the tables are still wrong. TTL limits damage per packet; the protocol design is what prevents loops. OSPF prevents them structurally; RIP merely suppresses them with split-horizon, poison reverse, and hold-downs.
Inside your own network, need fast convergence? → OSPF.
Talking to another organization — an ISP, a partner, the internet? → BGP.
Both offering the same prefix? → Someone configured redistribution. AD decides: lower wins.

GLANCE-AND-RECONSTRUCT
Facts converge; conclusions loop. OSPF trades stability for speed; BGP trades speed for stability. 10 · 20 · 110 · 200 — proximity to truth.
